Informal advising

Clinicians seeking engineering or chemistry expertise, and engineers seeking a clinical application for a technology, are welcome to contact us. Most collaborations with the Center begin this way.

Grant applications

Joint applications built around paired clinical and engineering teams. Teams with a record of prior collaboration are generally evaluated more favorably on investigators and environment.

Collaborations

Projects remain with their investigators. The Center coordinates collaborations and makes its facilities available to them.

Industry and foundations

The perianal fistula program is ready for translation, and we welcome discussion with potential partners. Enquiries about other programs are also welcome.

Patients and families

CTFI is a research center and cannot provide medical advice or arrange clinical care. Questions about Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis should be directed to your gastroenterologist.
