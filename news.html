  • 23 October 2026

    A paper in Science Advances describes an orally administered liposomal platform that carries tofacitinib, a JAK inhibitor approved for ulcerative colitis, to inflamed colon tissue, with the aim of limiting systemic exposure. In a dextran sodium sulfate model of colitis, a single oral dose outperformed daily dosing of free tofacitinib.
