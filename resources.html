Facilities and disease models

The Center's laboratories support chemistry, formulation, and endoscopic work in small and large animals at a single site, so that candidate compounds can progress from synthesis to large-animal studies without transfer between institutions.

Laboratory and instrumentation

Chemistry and chemical engineering laboratory

  • Agilent HPLC: purity, size-based separation, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics, stability, metabolism, drug–drug interaction and protein binding
  • Lyophilizer: freeze-drying for powdered preparations and long-term storage
  • Rotary evaporator: solvent removal without degradation of heat-sensitive compounds
  • Vacuum drying oven
  • Avanti extruder: lipid nanoparticle formulation with controlled particle size

Medicinal chemistry pipeline

  • Compound screening
  • Design of new compounds
  • Synthesis
  • Testing in vitro
  • Testing in vivo

Compounds that fail in vivo return directly to the design stage.

Small-animal endoscopy

  • Endoscopic submucosal injection in the rat rectum
  • A sleeve-assisted technique developed in the Center, in which a 3D-printed sleeve with a cut window exposes the rectal mucosa for controlled injection
  • Interim endoscopic assessment of stricture models

Large-animal endoscopy and surgery laboratory

  • Two-room suite: procedure room and preparation room
  • Equipped for large-animal endoscopy and surgery
  • C-arm fluoroscopy
  • Endoscopy tower
  • Adjustable table
  • Anesthesia machines and monitors

Small- and large-animal endoscopy

Disease models

Swine model of luminal stricture; rat models of stricture and of perianal fistula; intestinal transplant. Models are selected for intact physiology, including blood supply and luminal contents, and for endpoints with clinical relevance.
